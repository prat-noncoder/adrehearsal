    How the real version will work

    1. A vision AI watches your ad and writes a timeline of what is on screen and what is said.
    2. A panel is drawn from about 20,000 synthetic Indian people, each with an income band, a shopper type and a mood that day.
    3. Plain maths, not AI, decides who stops watching and when. The AI cannot flatter the result.
    4. An AI writes one short reason for each person who left, tied to that second.
    5. Everything simulated or assumed is labelled on the page.

    Built so far: the people bank and shopper types. Not connected yet: the ad-reading AI, the exit maths and live uploads. Code and history: github.com/prat-noncoder/adrehearsal
